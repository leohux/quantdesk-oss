# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in QuantDesk a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical trade-offs.
- Treat capital safety, credentials, and private trading data with care.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the project maintainer. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
